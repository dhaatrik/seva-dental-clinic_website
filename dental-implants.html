Dental Implants

The Gold Standard for a Permanent, Natural-Looking Smile

Rediscover the Confidence of a Full Smile

Losing a tooth can be a distressing experience, affecting not just your appearance but also your ability to eat and speak with confidence. Dental implants are the most advanced and reliable solution for replacing missing teeth, designed to look, feel, and function just like your natural teeth.

What is a Dental Implant?

A dental implant is a small, biocompatible titanium post that is surgically placed into the jawbone where a tooth is missing. This post acts as a new root for the tooth. Over time, the jawbone fuses with the implant in a process called osseointegration, creating a strong and stable foundation. A custom-made crown is then attached to the post, completing your new, fully functional tooth.

The Seva Dental Implant Process

  1. Consultation & Planning: Dr. Bhattacharya will conduct a thorough examination, including 3D imaging, to create a precise treatment plan tailored to you.
  2. Implant Placement: The titanium implant is gently placed into the jawbone in a comfortable, minimally invasive procedure.
  3. Healing & Integration: We allow a few months for the implant to fully integrate with your jawbone, ensuring a durable foundation.
  4. Final Restoration: Once healed, we attach your beautiful, custom-crafted crown, perfectly matching your other teeth.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the dental implant procedure painful?

Patients are often surprised at how little discomfort they experience. The procedure is performed under local anesthesia, and we provide comprehensive post-operative care instructions to manage any mild soreness.

How long do dental implants last?

With proper care and regular dental check-ups, dental implants can last a lifetime. They are a truly long-term investment in your oral health.

Am I a good candidate for dental implants?

Most people with missing teeth are good candidates. The primary requirement is having sufficient healthy jawbone to support the implant. We can determine this during your initial consultation.
