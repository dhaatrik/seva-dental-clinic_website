Preventative Care

Your First Step Towards a Lifetime of Healthy Smiles

Building a Foundation for Lifelong Oral Health

At Seva Dental Clinic, we believe that prevention is the best cure. Our preventative care services are designed to help you maintain a healthy, beautiful smile and avoid more extensive treatments in the future. Regular check-ups and professional cleanings are essential for removing plaque and tartar, detecting early signs of decay or gum disease, and receiving personalized advice on your oral hygiene routine.

What Does Preventative Care Include?

  • Routine Check-ups: Comprehensive examinations to assess your overall oral health, including teeth, gums, and soft tissues.
  • Professional Cleanings: Gentle removal of plaque and tartar buildup that cannot be removed by brushing and flossing alone.
  • Oral Cancer Screening: Early detection of potential issues.
  • Personalized Oral Hygiene Advice: Guidance on proper brushing, flossing, and other techniques to maintain optimal oral health at home.
  • Fluoride Treatments: Strengthening tooth enamel to prevent decay.
  • Sealants: Protecting vulnerable teeth (especially children's) from cavities.

Why is Preventative Care Important?

Preventative care not only helps you keep your natural teeth healthy but also contributes to your overall well-being. By addressing potential problems early, we can help you avoid pain, discomfort, and the need for more complex and costly treatments down the line. Regular visits allow us to monitor your oral health over time, identify any changes, and provide timely interventions.

Key Benefits

  • Early Detection of Problems
  • Prevents Tooth Decay & Gum Disease
  • Maintains a Healthy, Beautiful Smile
  • Saves Money on Future Treatments
  • Contributes to Overall Health

Frequently Asked Questions

How often should I have a dental check-up?

For most people, we recommend a check-up and cleaning every six months. However, Dr. Bhattacharya may recommend more frequent visits based on your individual oral health needs.

What happens during a routine check-up?

During a check-up, we'll examine your teeth and gums for any signs of decay, gum disease, or other problems. We'll also perform a professional cleaning to remove plaque and tartar. Dr. Bhattacharya will discuss any findings and provide personalized recommendations.

Is preventative care only for adults?

No! Preventative care is essential for people of all ages, including children. Establishing good oral hygiene habits early in life is crucial for preventing future dental problems.
