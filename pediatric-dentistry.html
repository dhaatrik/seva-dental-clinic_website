Pediatric Dentistry

Making Dental Care a Fun Adventure for Our Youngest Explorers

Building a Foundation for a Lifetime of Healthy Smiles

At Seva Dental Clinic, we believe that positive experiences in early childhood set the stage for a lifetime of good oral health. Our pediatric dentistry services are specially designed to create a fun, comfortable, and educational environment for children. Dr. Bhattacharya and our caring team use gentle techniques and age-appropriate communication to help children feel at ease during their dental visits.

Our Approach to Pediatric Dentistry

We focus on prevention, early detection, and building trust with our young patients. Our goal is to make each visit an enjoyable adventure, teaching children the importance of oral hygiene and empowering them to take care of their smiles.

Services We Offer

  • Routine Check-ups & Cleanings: Gentle examinations and cleanings to monitor growth and development, detect any issues early, and remove plaque and tartar.
  • Fluoride Treatments: Strengthening tooth enamel to prevent cavities.
  • Dental Sealants: Applying a protective coating to the chewing surfaces of back teeth to seal out food and bacteria.
  • Cavity Fillings: Using tooth-colored filling materials to repair cavities and restore the tooth's natural appearance.
  • Space Maintainers: If a child loses a baby tooth prematurely, we may use a space maintainer to keep the space open for the permanent tooth to erupt correctly.
  • Oral Hygiene Education: Teaching children proper brushing and flossing techniques in a fun and engaging way.
  • Dietary Counseling: Providing guidance on healthy food choices that support strong teeth.
  • Management of Dental Emergencies: Prompt and compassionate care for dental injuries or toothaches.

Frequently Asked Questions

When should my child have their first dental visit?

We recommend bringing your child for their first dental visit by their first birthday or within six months after their first tooth erupts, whichever comes first.

How can I prepare my child for their first visit?

Talk to your child about the visit in a positive and exciting way. You can read books about going to the dentist or role-play a dental visit at home. Avoid using any negative words like "pain" or "shot."

What are dental sealants?

Sealants are a thin, protective coating painted on the chewing surfaces of the back teeth (molars) to prevent cavities. They are a simple and painless way to protect your child's teeth from decay.
