Teeth Whitening

A Brighter, More Confident Smile Awaits

Unlock Your Brightest Smile Safely and Effectively

Over time, it's natural for teeth to become discolored due to factors like coffee, tea, red wine, and aging. Professional teeth whitening is one of the simplest and most effective ways to enhance your smile and boost your confidence. At Seva Dental Clinic, we offer safe, professional-grade whitening solutions that deliver noticeable results far superior to over-the-counter products.

Why Choose Professional Whitening?

While store-bought whitening kits are available, professional treatment offers significant advantages. We use stronger, more effective whitening agents in a controlled and safe environment. Dr. Bhattacharya will first ensure your teeth and gums are healthy enough for whitening, protecting you from potential sensitivity and damage.

Our Whitening Options

  • In-Office Whitening: For the fastest and most dramatic results. In a single visit, we can brighten your smile by several shades using a powerful, light-activated whitening gel. This is perfect for special occasions or anyone wanting immediate improvement.
  • Custom Take-Home Kits: We provide you with custom-fitted trays and professional-strength whitening gel. This allows you to whiten your teeth gradually and conveniently in the comfort of your own home, with professional guidance.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is teeth whitening safe?

Yes, when performed by a dental professional, teeth whitening is very safe. We take precautions to protect your gums and ensure the procedure is right for your oral health.

How long do the results last?

The results can last for several years, depending on your diet and oral hygiene habits. Avoiding staining foods and drinks will help maintain your bright smile for longer.

Will teeth whitening make my teeth sensitive?

Some patients may experience temporary sensitivity, but this usually subsides quickly. We use desensitizing agents and techniques to minimize any discomfort during and after the treatment.
