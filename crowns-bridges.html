Crowns & Bridges

Restoring Your Smile's Strength and Beauty

Rebuild and Complete Your Smile with Custom Solutions

Whether you have a damaged tooth or a missing one, dental crowns and bridges are reliable and aesthetically pleasing solutions to restore your smile's health, function, and appearance. At Seva Dental Clinic, we use high-quality materials to craft custom restorations that blend seamlessly with your natural teeth.

What is a Dental Crown?

A dental crown, often called a "cap," is a custom-made covering that fits over a damaged tooth. It restores the tooth's shape, size, strength, and appearance. Crowns are recommended to:

  • Protect a weak tooth from breaking or hold together parts of a cracked tooth.
  • Restore an already broken tooth or a tooth that has been severely worn down.
  • Cover and support a tooth with a large filling when there isn't a lot of tooth left.
  • Cover a tooth that has had a root canal.
  • Cover a dental implant.

What is a Dental Bridge?

A dental bridge is used to "bridge the gap" created by one or more missing teeth. It is made up of two or more crowns for the teeth on either side of the gap (these are the anchoring teeth) and a false tooth/teeth in between. A bridge restores your bite and helps keep the natural shape of your face.

Frequently Asked Questions

What are crowns and bridges made of?

They can be made from various materials, including porcelain, ceramic, zirconia, and metal alloys. We will discuss the best option for you based on the tooth's location, function, and your aesthetic preferences.

How long do crowns and bridges last?

With good oral hygiene and regular check-ups, crowns and bridges can last for many years, often 10-15 years or even longer. Durability depends on the material used and your personal habits.

How do I care for my crown or bridge?

Caring for them is just like caring for your natural teeth. It's important to maintain a routine of brushing twice a day, flossing daily (we'll show you how to floss under a bridge), and keeping up with regular dental check-ups.
